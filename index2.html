I'm a PhD student in Human-Centered Computing at UMBC. My research looks at how people feel when they reflect on their experiences, express themselves through writing, drawing and movement, and take in the world through their screens, and what interactive systems could do with that. I will be exploring the intersection of affective computing and emotional well-being through the lenses of collaborative reflection and dance therapy.
